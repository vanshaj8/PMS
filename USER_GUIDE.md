## 1. Introduction

### What This Application Does

The PIP Management System is a digital tool that helps organizations manage Performance Improvement Plans for employees. Think of it as a smart filing cabinet that:

- Keeps track of employee performance improvement plans
- Guides managers, employees, and HR through a structured process
- Stores all documents and communications in one place
- Sends reminders about important deadlines
- Generates reports for management

### Who Should Use It

**Managers**: Create and manage PIPs for your team members, review employee progress, and make recommendations.

**Employees**: View your PIP, acknowledge it, track your progress, and submit self-reviews.

**HR Business Partners (HRBP)**: Review PIPs, approve or send them back for changes, and make final decisions.

**Administrators**: Manage users, import employee data, view system-wide reports, and maintain the system.

**Executives**: View dashboards and reports to understand organizational performance trends.

### What Problems It Solves

- **Organization**: No more lost paperwork or confusion about where a PIP stands
- **Timeline Tracking**: Automatic reminders ensure nothing falls through the cracks
- **Transparency**: Everyone involved can see the current status and next steps
- **Consistency**: All PIPs follow the same structured process
- **Documentation**: Everything is saved automatically and can be accessed anytime

## 2. How the System Works

### Simple Explanation

Imagine the system as a digital workflow:

1. **You click a button** → The system saves your action
2. **You fill out a form** → The information is stored securely
3. **You submit something** → The right people are notified
4. **Deadlines approach** → The system sends reminders

### What Happens When You Click a Button

When you click any button in the system:

1. The system checks if you're allowed to perform that action
2. It saves your information
3. It notifies the next person in the workflow (if needed)
4. It updates the status of the PIP
5. It shows you a confirmation message

### How Data is Saved and Retrieved

- **Saving**: Every time you click "Save" or "Submit," your information is automatically stored. You don't need to worry about losing your work.

- **Retrieving**: When you open a page, the system automatically loads the latest information. Everything you see is up-to-date.

- **Security**: Only people with the right permissions can see and edit information. Your data is protected.

### Feature: Create a New PIP (Managers Only)

**Description**: Start a new Performance Improvement Plan for an employee.

**When to Use It**: 
- When you need to create a formal improvement plan for an employee
- After discussing performance issues with the employee
- When HR has approved moving forward with a PIP

**Steps to Use:**

1. **Click "Create PIP" or "Initiate PIP"**
   - This is usually a button on the dashboard or PIP list page

2. **Select Employee**
   - Choose the employee from the dropdown list
   - Only active employees will appear

3. **Select HRBP**
   - Choose the HR Business Partner who will review this PIP
   - This person will approve or request changes

4. **Enter Reason**
   - Explain why this PIP is being created
   - Be clear and specific about the performance concerns

5. **Add Supporting Documents (Optional)**
   - Upload any relevant documents
   - These might include previous reviews, emails, or other evidence

6. **Set Goals**
   - For each goal, provide:
     - **Title**: What needs to be improved
     - **Description**: Detailed explanation
     - **Weightage**: How important this goal is (as a percentage)
     - **Expected Outcome**: What success looks like
     - **Target Timeline**: When this should be achieved
     - **Deadline**: Specific date
   - Click "Add Goal" to add more goals
   - Total weightage should add up to 100%

7. **Set Timeline**
   - **Employee Acknowledgement Deadline**: When the employee must acknowledge
   - **PIP Active Duration**: How long the PIP will be active (in days)
   - **Employee Self-Review Deadline**: When the employee must submit their review
   - **Manager Final Review Deadline**: When you must complete your review
   - **HRBP Final Decision Deadline**: When HRBP must make the final decision

8. **Review and Submit**
   - Review all information
   - Click "Submit" or "Create PIP"

**Result**: 
- The PIP is created and sent to HRBP for review
- HRBP will receive a notification
- You'll see a confirmation message
- The PIP appears in your list with status "Pending HRBP Review"

4. **Submit Your Review**
   - Review all your responses
   - Click "Submit Self-Review"

**Result**: 
- Your review is submitted to your manager
- Status changes to "Pending Manager Review"
- Your manager will review your progress

**Description**: Managers review employee self-reviews and provide feedback.

**When to Use It**: 
- After an employee submits their self-review

   - For each goal, select a status:

4. **Add Overall Comments**

   - Note any patterns or concerns

5. **Submit Your Review**
   - Review all your assessments

**Result**: 
- Your review is sent to HRBP
- Status changes to "Pending HRBP Decision"

### Feature: HRBP Final Decision (HRBP Only)

**Description**: HR Business Partners make the final decision on PIP outcomes.

**When to Use It**: 
- After both employee self-review and manager review are complete
- When you receive a notification about a pending decision
- Before the HRBP decision deadline

**Steps to Use:**

1. **Open the PIP**
   - Go to your PIP list
   - Find PIPs with status "Pending HRBP Decision"
   - Click to open the one you need to review

2. **Review Everything**
   - Read the original PIP goals
   - Review the employee's self-review
   - Review the manager's assessment
   - Consider all evidence and documentation

3. **Make Your Decision**
   - Select the final outcome:
     - **Successful**: Employee has met the requirements
     - **Unsuccessful**: Employee has not met the requirements
     - **Extended**: PIP period needs to be extended
     - **Closed Without Action**: PIP is closed for other reasons

4. **Add Final Remarks**
   - Explain your decision
   - Provide any additional context
   - Note any follow-up actions needed

5. **Submit Decision**
   - Review your decision and remarks
   - Click "Submit Final Decision"

**Result**: 
- The PIP is marked as "Completed"
- The PIP is locked (no further changes)
- All parties are notified of the outcome
- The decision is recorded permanently

## 6. Common User Flows

### Flow 1: Manager Creates a PIP

**Step 1**: Manager logs in and goes to dashboard

**Step 2**: Manager clicks "Create PIP" or "Initiate PIP"

**Step 3**: Manager fills out the form:
- Selects employee
- Selects HRBP
- Enters reason
- Adds goals
- Sets timeline

**Step 4**: Manager clicks "Submit"

**Step 5**: System sends PIP to HRBP for review

**Step 6**: Manager sees confirmation and PIP appears in list with "Pending HRBP Review" status

---

### Flow 2: HRBP Reviews and Approves PIP

**Step 1**: HRBP receives notification about new PIP

**Step 2**: HRBP opens the PIP and reviews all information

**Step 3**: HRBP decides:
- **Approve**: PIP moves to employee for acknowledgement
- **Request Changes**: PIP goes back to manager with comments
- **Reject**: PIP is cancelled (if this option exists)

**Step 4**: If approved, status changes to "Pending Employee Acknowledgement"

**Step 5**: Employee receives notification

---

### Flow 3: Employee Acknowledges and Works on PIP

**Step 1**: Employee receives notification about new PIP

**Step 2**: Employee opens PIP and reviews goals and timeline

**Step 3**: Employee clicks "Acknowledge"

**Step 4**: Status changes to "Active" - improvement period begins

**Step 5**: Employee works on goals, attends check-ins with manager

**Step 6**: When self-review deadline approaches, employee submits self-review

---

### Flow 4: Complete Review Cycle

**Step 1**: Employee submits self-review before deadline

**Step 2**: Status changes to "Pending Manager Review"

**Step 3**: Manager receives notification

**Step 4**: Manager reviews employee's self-review and submits assessment

**Step 5**: Status changes to "Pending HRBP Decision"

**Step 6**: HRBP receives notification

**Step 7**: HRBP reviews everything and makes final decision

**Step 8**: Status changes to "Completed" and PIP is locked

**Step 9**: All parties receive notification of final outcome

## 7. Errors & Messages

### Common Error Messages

**"Cannot connect to server"**
- **What it means**: The system can't reach the server
- **What to do**: 
  - Check your internet connection
  - Try refreshing the page
  - Contact IT support if problem persists

**"Invalid email or password"**
- **What it means**: Your login credentials are incorrect
- **What to do**: 
  - Check that you're using the correct email
  - Make sure Caps Lock is off
  - Try typing password again
  - Contact administrator to reset password

**"You do not have permission to perform this action"**
- **What it means**: Your role doesn't allow this action
- **What to do**: 
  - This action might be for a different role
  - Contact administrator if you believe you should have access
  - Check if you're on the right PIP (some actions are role-specific)

**"Required field is missing"**
- **What it means**: You haven't filled in all required information
- **What to do**: 
  - Look for red asterisks (*) or highlighted fields
  - Fill in all required fields
  - Check that dropdowns have selections

**"Deadline has passed"**
- **What it means**: You're trying to take an action after the deadline
- **What to do**: 
  - Contact your manager or HRBP
  - They may be able to extend the deadline
  - Document why the deadline was missed

**"PIP is locked"**
- **What it means**: The PIP has been completed and can't be changed
- **What to do**: 
  - This is normal for completed PIPs
  - You can still view the PIP but not edit it
  - Contact administrator if you believe changes are needed

### Success Messages

**"PIP created successfully"**
- Your PIP has been created and sent for review

**"Review submitted successfully"**
- Your review has been saved and sent to the next person

**"Changes saved"**
- Your updates have been saved

**"User created successfully"**
- The new user account has been created

### Warning Messages

**"You have pending actions"**
- You have PIPs that need your attention
- Check your dashboard or PIP list

**"Deadline approaching"**
- A deadline is coming up soon
- Take action before it's too late

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: What is a PIP?**
A: A Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) is a structured process to help employees improve their performance. It includes specific goals, timelines, and regular check-ins.

**Q: Who can see my PIP?**
A: Only people directly involved: you (the employee), your manager, the assigned HRBP, and administrators. Other employees cannot see your PIP.

**Q: Can I edit a PIP after it's created?**
A: Once a PIP is created, only certain people can make changes at certain stages. After completion, PIPs are locked and cannot be changed. Contact your manager or HRBP if you need changes.

**Q: What if I miss a deadline?**
A: Contact your manager or HRBP immediately. They may be able to extend the deadline or help you catch up.

**Q: Can I delete a PIP?**
A: No, PIPs cannot be deleted once created. This ensures a complete record. If a PIP was created in error, contact an administrator.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What browser should I use?**
A: The system works best with Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge. Make sure your browser is up to date.

**Q: Can I use the system on my phone?**
A: Yes, the system is designed to work on mobile devices, though some features may be easier to use on a computer.

**Q: What if the page doesn't load?**
A: Try refreshing the page. If that doesn't work, check your internet connection. Contact IT support if the problem continues.

**Q: How do I upload documents?**
A: Click "Choose File" or drag and drop files into the upload area. Supported formats usually include PDF, Word, Excel, and images.

**Q: Can I save my work and come back later?**
A: Yes, many forms have a "Save" or "Save as Draft" option. However, some actions (like submitting a review) are final once you click "Submit."

### Role-Specific Questions

**Q: As an employee, what should I do if I disagree with my PIP?**
A: First, discuss your concerns with your manager. If needed, you can involve HRBP. You can also add comments when acknowledging the PIP.

**Q: As a manager, can I create a PIP for multiple employees at once?**
A: No, each PIP is created individually for one employee. This ensures each PIP is tailored to that person's specific situation.

**Q: As HRBP, what if I need more information before making a decision?**
A: You can send the PIP back to the manager with a request for changes or additional information. Add comments explaining what you need.

**Q: As an administrator, how do I reset someone's password?**
A: Go to User Management, find the user, click Edit, and set a new password. The user should change it after first login.

## 10. Tips & Best Practices

### For All Users

**Check Notifications Regularly**
- The notification bell shows important updates
- Check it daily to stay on top of deadlines
- Don't ignore deadline warnings

**Read Everything Carefully**
- Take time to read PIP goals and requirements
- Understand deadlines before they approach
- Ask questions if anything is unclear

- Keep supporting documents ready
- Don't wait until the last minute to gather evidence

**Communicate Proactively**
- If you're going to miss a deadline, communicate early
- Ask for help if you're unsure about something
- Provide updates even if not required

### For Employees

**Take PIPs Seriously**
- A PIP is an opportunity to improve, not a punishment
- Engage actively in the process
- Be honest in your self-reviews
- Document your progress

**Prepare for Reviews**
- Start working on goals immediately
- Keep notes of your progress
- Gather evidence of achievements
- Don't wait until the deadline to start your self-review

**Ask for Support**
- If you're struggling with goals, talk to your manager
- Request additional resources if needed
- Use check-ins to discuss challenges

**Be Clear and Specific**
- Write clear, measurable goals
- Set realistic timelines
- Provide examples of what success looks like
- Be available for questions

**Provide Regular Feedback**
- Don't wait for formal reviews
- Use check-ins to give ongoing feedback
- Celebrate progress, not just final outcomes
- Be constructive and supportive

**Review Objectively**
- Base assessments on evidence
- Consider the employee's self-review fairly
- Provide specific, actionable feedback
- Be consistent across all employees

### For HRBPs

**Review Thoroughly**
- Read all documentation before making decisions
- Consider both employee and manager perspectives
- Look for patterns or concerns
- Ensure the process was followed correctly

**Make Timely Decisions**
- Don't delay decisions unnecessarily
- Communicate clearly about your reasoning
- Be consistent in your assessments
- Document your decisions well

- Help troubleshoot issues
